Questions

Does it really run silent?

For everyday work the fans stay off. They only spin up under long, heavy loads, and even then they stay low and even in tone.

How fast does it charge?

A 100 W USB-C charger takes it from empty to about 50% in 25 minutes and to full in under an hour.

Can I upgrade the memory later?

Memory is built into the chip package, so choose the amount you need at checkout. Storage goes up to 4 TB.

What comes in the box?

The laptop, a 100 W USB-C charger, a braided USB-C cable and a soft sleeve.
